About

Our Mission

Every great game deserves a second life. At Blackwall Studio, we believe that the best multiplayer experiences aren't always the ones planned from day one — sometimes they emerge from the passion of communities who refuse to let a game fade into obscurity.

We specialize in retro-fitting multiplayer into games that shipped without it, developing custom networking solutions, dedicated server infrastructure, and seamless matchmaking systems that feel like they were always part of the original design.

From underground modding scenes to full-scale studio partnerships, our work is defined by one principle: games should be shared.

What We Do

Our Capabilities

From reverse engineering netcode to building full-scale multiplayer ecosystems — we bring the tools, talent, and tenacity to make any game social.

🔌

Netcode & Multiplayer Engineering

Custom networking solutions built from the ground up. Client-server architectures, peer-to-peer rollback, dedicated server infrastructure, and seamless matchmaking tailored to each game's unique engine and design.

🔄

Game Preservation & Revival

Bringing abandoned or single-player-only games back to life with modern multiplayer capabilities. Server emulation, protocol reverse engineering, and community-hosted infrastructure for games your players refuse to forget.

⚡

Modding SDKs & Tools

Developing modding frameworks, plugin APIs, and SDKs that empower communities to extend their favorite games. From Redscript to Lua — we build the toolchain so creators can build the world.

🌐

Dedicated Server Infrastructure

Scalable, containerized server orchestration for game backends. Auto-scaling matchmaking, region-based deployment, anti-cheat integration, and real-time telemetry so your players always find a low-ping game.

🎮

Cross-Platform Multiplayer

Bridging communities across PC, console, and cloud. Unified accounts, cross-save, cross-play matchmaking, and platform-agnostic party systems that keep friends together regardless of hardware.

🤝

Studio Partnership & Consulting

End-to-end consultation for studios adding multiplayer post-launch. Architecture review, network design docs, load testing, latency optimization, and go-to-market strategy for your game's online future.
